Accounts, AI and appearance

Accounts

The bank and card accounts your statements go into.

Maintenance

Tidy-up jobs for statements you imported before iSpend got better at reading merchant names.
Re-detect merchant names
Goes back over every charge and tidies up the merchant name, dropping cardholder names, payment codes and branch numbers. Your rules and remembered merchants follow the new names.
Learn from history
Looks back over everything you have sorted so far so the next statement recognises it. This happens on its own as you go — use it after changing a lot at once.

Saved file layouts

Column mappings remembered from CSV and Excel files you imported. The next file with the same columns is mapped the same way and goes into the same account. Delete one to map that file by hand next time.

AI assistance

Optional. Add a key and iSpend can suggest categories for charges it doesn’t recognise, and write up what it notices each month. Suggestions are never applied without your say-so, and this setting is yours alone.

Appearance

The look, the spacing and the currency are remembered in this browser and on your account.
